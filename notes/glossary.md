# Glossary, in my own words

| Term | My explanation | Example |
|------|----------------|---------|
| Gene |A specific segment of DNA located inside a cell's nucleus. It carries the instructions for building molecules (mostly proteins) that run the body and determine traits| A, T, C, and G |
| Gene expression / RNA |When the gene is making a mRNA copy, we say that the gene is expressed (transcription), then translation in the ribosome happens which reads the mRNA to build protein. RNA (one strand) is basically copies instruction from the DNA (two strand) |A cell in the pancreas expresses the insulin gene, so it makes lots of insulin mRNA.
A skin cell has the same insulin gene in its DNA but doesn't express it, so it makes zero insulin mRNA |
| Cell type vs cell state |What the cell is vs what the cell does |T cell, B cell, monocyte, neuron, liver cell. Cell state is caused by what's happening around the cell: infection, stress, signals from other cells, the cell cycle |
| scRNA-seq |To find out which genes are active in the cells we read and count its mRNA using a sequence machine | A tumor isn't just one type of cell. By reading and counting the mRNA in thousands of individual tumor cells with a sequencing machine, doctors can spot the tiny 1% of cells that have 'drug-resistance' genes turned on—and choose a treatment that targets them.|
